The idea

From woodland floor to finished piece.

We work one private oak woodland near Nottingham, by permission of its owner, helping clear timber that has already come down — storm‑thrown, wind‑snapped, or simply old. No tree is ever cut standing.

That oak is seasoned slowly, then shaped entirely by hand into pieces that keep the character of the tree they came from. Nothing is repeated, because nothing we start with can be.

What we add beyond the craft is consistency: every piece leaves our workshop finished to the same standard, ready for your shelves this Christmas.

How a piece is made

Four stages, one at a time.

01

Gathered, not felled

We walk the woodland with the owner, selecting oak that has already come down. Nothing standing is ever touched.

02

Seasoned slowly

Air‑dried for many months so the timber settles honestly before it is ever shaped.

03

Made by hand

Cut, worked and finished by hand in our workshop, one piece at a time, never as a batch.

04

Finished & packed

Hand-finished, checked, and carefully packed for despatch — ready for your shop floor or showroom.

Who's behind it

Two people, one wood, one workshop.

Velique is run by two friends who would rather make one unrepeatable piece a week than a hundred identical ones a day. Every stage — sourcing, seasoning, making, finishing — stays under our own roof.
